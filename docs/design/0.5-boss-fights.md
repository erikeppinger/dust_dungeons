# Design draft: Boss fights (0.5)

> Status: **core decisions made** (§11) · 2026-10-28
> Our own design. It is inspired by the *rhythm-combat genre* in general and copies no specific game:
> no characters, art, music, names or level designs from existing titles.

---

## 1. Goal

Points and XP finally become **usable**. Finished chores give you **fight tokens**. A token lets you fight a
boss that embodies that chore ("Dust Bunny King", "Laundry Hydra"). Fights are short, optional and
playable whenever you like, so logging a chore never requires playing.

**Design principles**
1. **Chores feed the game, the game never replaces chores.** Points, XP and tokens come only from chores.
   Fights give trophies and cosmetics, never spendable points, so playing can't stand in for doing chores.
2. **Short:** one fight lasts 30–60 seconds, so it fits between two chores.
3. **Forgiving:** losing costs nothing but the token's fight. Story mode can't be lost.
4. **Accessible by design:** playable without sound, without color vision and with slow reactions (§8).
5. **No external assets needed:** bosses are drawn in code from shapes, and beats are synthesized, so there
   are no licensing problems and the app stays small and offline.

---

## 2. Core loop

```
 do chore ──▶ +points +XP (as today)  +1 fight token (for that chore's boss family)
                                              │
                    whenever you like ◀───────┘
                                              ▼
                     World tab: pick a boss ─▶ fight (30–60 s) ─▶ win: trophy, boss "cleaned"
                                              │                    lose: try again with another token
                     points ─▶ shop: gear that changes how fights play (never stronger than "skill")
                     XP level ─▶ hero HP and damage grow slowly
                     5 bosses beaten this week ─▶ the weekly "Chore Overlord" appears
```

---

## 3. Fight tokens

- Every completed chore grants **1 token** of its **boss family** (see §5).
- **Undo** removes the token again. If it was already spent, the family's balance goes negative and the
  next token of that family pays the debt back. That's simple, fair and can't be exploited.
- Tokens don't expire. *(The planned cap of 20 per family was dropped in 0.5.0: a derived balance can't be capped cleanly.)*
- Stored as data, not as a counter: tokens = completions − fights started (per family), like the
  point ledger. That keeps it auditable and makes undo trivial.

---

## 4. The fight: "Echo duel" (proposed mechanic)

A **call-and-response rhythm duel** on one big pad, easy to learn and very readable:

1. **Call:** the boss "plays" a short rhythm, 2–6 beats on a steady pulse. Each beat is shown as a shape
   flashing on the pad and played as a tone, and the phone also vibrates on each beat.
2. **Echo:** you tap the pad in the same rhythm.
   - *Perfect* (±60 ms) → full hit, *Good* (±120 ms) → half hit, *Miss* → the boss's attack lands.
3. Hits fill the **cleaning meter** on the boss; when it is full, the boss is "cleaned" (won).
   Misses cost hero HP; at 0 HP the fight is lost.
4. Patterns get longer and syncopated as the boss loses HP (2–3 phases per boss).

Why this mechanic:
- It works with **one thumb**, on any screen size, and needs no lanes or fast eye movement.
- **Sound is optional:** the visual pulse and vibration carry the rhythm on their own.
- It scales from very easy (2 slow beats) to hard (6 fast, off-beat) just by changing patterns, which is ideal
  for tying difficulty to the chore's points.
- It's cheap to build: one pad, a pattern player and a timing judge. That's mostly pure logic, so it's testable.

**Decided: a mix.** The echo duel is the mechanic for all regular bosses. The weekly **Chore Overlord** adds
a special phase with **three lanes of falling notes**, a change of pace for the big fight of the week. The lane
phase gets the same accessibility options (story mode, slow mode, haptics, shapes instead of colors).

## 5. Bosses

Each built-in chore belongs to a family. User chores get the **Clutter Imp** unless the user picks a family in
the editor.

| Family | Chores | Boss (working names) | Pattern flavor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dust | dusting, sweeping, vacuuming, mopping | **Dust Bunny King** | light, bouncy, even beats |
| Laundry | laundry, hang, fold, ironing | **Laundry Hydra** (one head per phase) | repeated motifs, growing |
| Kitchen | dishes, dishwasher, kitchen, fridge | **Sink Kraken** | rolling triplets |
| Bath | bathroom, toilet | **Porcelain Golem** | slow, heavy, long gaps |
| Trash | trash, recycling | **Trash Titan** | stomping, off-beat |
| Bedroom | change bed sheets, tidy up | **Pillow Phantom** | soft, swung rhythm |
| Outside & errands | windows, plants, groceries | **Window Wraith** | quick bursts |
| Other | user chores | **Clutter Imp** | random mix |

**Weekly boss: Chore Overlord.** It appears after 5 won fights in the week (Mon–Sun). It has 3 phases: two echo
phases combining motifs of the bosses you beat, then a final **lane phase** (falling notes in 3 lanes).
Beating it gives the week's crown trophy.

**Difficulty** *(as built in 0.5.0)*: the tier rises with how often you have beaten that boss (tier 2 from
3 wins, tier 3 from 8). Higher tiers play faster, longer patterns, and the boss has more health. Tying the tier
to the chore's points is still open for later.

## 6. Progression and economy

| Source | Gives | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Chore | points, XP, 1 token | (as today) |
| XP level | +HP and +damage per level (small) | survive longer fights |
| Points | currency | **gear** and **avatar items** in the shop |
| Fight won | trophy, boss "cleaned" for the day, cosmetic shards | trophy shelf, cosmetics |

**Gear** changes how fights play, not just numbers (first ideas):
- **Sponge Shield:** forgives the first miss of each fight.
- **Turbo Mop:** a *Perfect* deals 1.5× damage.
- **Metronome Charm:** wider timing windows (also an accessibility aid, so it's cheap).
- **Rubber Gloves:** +1 max HP.
- **Second Wind** (consumable): continue once after losing.

**Avatar (decided):** a simple hero drawn in code with customizable colors and accessories (hats, capes,
cleaning tools as weapons). Basic options are free, and extra items are bought with points. This gives points a
second, purely fun use next to gear.

Prices from roughly 50 to 500 points. Spending points never lowers XP or level (already true today).

**The World tab becomes the hub:** your hero, boss families with their token counts, the trophy shelf,
the shop and the Overlord's progress.

## 7. Art and sound

- **Bosses drawn in code** (Flutter `CustomPainter` / Flame components) from simple shapes with a face and
  two or three animation poses. They use the chore palette, which supports the colorblind mode, and every
  boss also has a distinct **silhouette**, so color is never needed to tell them apart.
- **Sound (decided: on by default, with haptics):** short percussive tones synthesized at build time by a
  script in `tool/`, so we own them and they are CC0. No music licensing. A soft loop per family is optional
  later. Sound and haptics can each be switched off in the fight settings.
- **Haptics** on each beat, on by default.

---

## 8. Accessibility

- Every beat is **visual + haptic + audio**, and any one of the three is enough.
- **Story mode:** you can't lose; misses only slow the cleaning meter.
- **Timing assist:** wider windows; **slow mode:** 70 % tempo.
- **Latency calibration** (tap along to 8 beats once) so Bluetooth headphones don't ruin timing.
- **Reduced motion** respects the system setting (no screen shake or flashes).
- TalkBack: the menus are fully labeled. The fight itself announces the call ("3 beats") and supports a
  simplified "tap when it vibrates" mode.

---

## 9. Technical plan

- **Engine** *(as built in 0.5.0)*: **plain Flutter** (`FightController` with timers and the `clock`
  package, so tests run on simulated time). The echo duel doesn't need a game engine. Flame stays an option
  for the lane phase in 0.5.2. The judging logic (patterns, timing, damage) lives in `lib/domain/fight/` as
  **pure Dart**, so it's fully unit-testable without rendering.
- **Audio** *(as built)*: `audioplayers` with low-latency `AudioPool`s (MIT, adds no permissions). The sounds
  are generated by `tool/generate_sounds.py`.
- **Known limit until 0.5.2:** no latency calibration yet. On phones with slow audio output, the count-in click
  is heard late. Story mode's wider timing helps until then.
- **Data (schema v4):**
  - `Fights` (id, profileId, bossFamily, bossTier, startedAt, endedAt, result win/lose/abandoned, accuracy)
  - `Inventory` (profileId, itemKey, count)
  - tokens are derived (§3), and `bossFamily` is a new nullable column on `Chores` (null = from seed/default)
- **Ledger:** buying gear books a negative `spend` entry in `PointLedger` (balance drops, XP doesn't).

## 11. Decisions (2026-10-28)

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| Mechanic | **Mix:** echo duel for regular bosses, a falling-note lane phase for the weekly Overlord |
| Rewards for winning | **Trophies and cosmetics only.** Fights never give spendable points |
| Sound | **On by default, with haptics**, and each can be turned off |
| Hero | **Customizable avatar**: free basics, extra items bought with points |
| Fight tokens | One per completed chore, fights optional and playable any time (decided earlier) |

**Still open:** final app name. If boss fights stay, the name could follow ("Chore Quest" is taken by
existing apps, so any name needs a check; ideas: *Dust & Glory*, *Sparkle Smash*, *Haushelden*).
